For anyone with an interest in the long-term health and longevity of the
broader ecosystem of Postgres extensions and tools, we'd like to hear your
ideas and inspirations for its assurance. Come to the [PGConf.EU] half-day
[Community Events Day] session in València this year, cheekily called [A
Postgres Ecosystem Foundation?][pgef]:

> There are so many projects around Postgres which we rely on to extend
> functionality of the database engine, connect applications to the database,
> or simplify management and operations. Join us for an open discussion of the
> recent activity around developing the health of the ecosystem and what could
> be done to improve it further.

I'll lead off with with a brief history of the work to create a "Postgres
Ecosystem Foundation". Then [Peter Eisentraut] will pitch some ideas to start
organically building support for ecosystem projects and authors. [Floor
Drees], [Alastair Turner], and [Mark Wong] will lay out potential topics and
themes we missed. A few top-of-mind candidates from yours truly:

*   How to ensure the long-term viability of ecosystem projects
*   What to do about abandoned projects
*   How to share effort and cost amongst interested parties in the maintenance
    of ecosystem projects
*   What resources and services would best support ecosystem projects for the
    long term
*   How avoid license claw-back
*   What to do for maintainer retirement
*   How do we mentor and grow new maintainers
*   Increase visibility of projects
*   Raise and distribute funds for project maintenance
*   Provide additional community services like security scanning or packaging

Bring your own list! We'll then organize around questions and concerns to open
the floor for discussion.

We want your input!

We plan to take your input to start to develop concrete plans and socialize
them to the rest of the community in the coming months. But the best place to
get in early input will be this session.

[Please join us][pgef]!
